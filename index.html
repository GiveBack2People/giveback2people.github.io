Our Ethos

Why GiveBack2People?

We believe that high-quality knowledge and tools shouldn't be hidden behind paywalls or lost in obscure bookmark folders.

Open Access for Everyone

All collections, repositories, and documentation published under GiveBack2People are free and open source under permissive licenses.

Curated Quality, Zero Spam

We fight link rot and low-value directories by rigorously checking every single tool, keeping listings maintained and cleanly categorized.

Community-Driven

Every initiative is built with contributor-friendly guidelines, transparent issues, and automated CI pipelines to make contributing a pleasure.

Help Us Pay It Forward

GiveBack2People thrives on community contributions. Whether you found a new favorite tool, noticed a broken link, or have a project idea, your voice matters.
